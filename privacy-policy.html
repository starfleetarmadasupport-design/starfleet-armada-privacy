1. Overview

Starfleet Armada Pinball (“we”, “our”, or the “Game”) is a free, ad-supported single-player 3D arcade pinball game (with an optional “Remove Ads” in-app upgrade planned for future/region-supported releases). We do not operate first-party user accounts, login servers, or cloud player profiles.

This Privacy Policy explains what data is stored locally on your device, what data may be processed by third-party Google services (Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, and Google Play Billing), and how you can manage your privacy choices.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide core gameplay and remember your preferences, the Game stores the following data strictly in local device storage (localStorage):

  • High Score & Gameplay Progress: Your local Grand Champion high score.
  • Game & Graphics Settings: Audio mute state, soundtrack (BGM) toggle, haptics toggle, graphics quality preset (auto, low, medium, high), and camera perspective preference.
  • Monetization & Consent State:
    • Local “Remove Ads” entitlement status (remove_ads).
    • Cached UMP consent status and interstitial cooldown timestamp.

This local data stays on your device and is not transmitted to any first-party developer server. Clearing app data or uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored information.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

The free, ad-supported version of Starfleet Armada Pinball uses Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display between-game interstitials, cabinet lower-apron placements, and optional rewarded video advertisements (as well as banner placements where enabled).

Depending on your region and consent choices, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID) and app set IDs.
  • Device & Network Diagnostics: IP address (used for coarse regional ad delivery and fraud prevention), device model, OS version, screen dimensions, and app version.
  • Ad Interaction & Performance Metrics: Ad impressions, views, clicks, and crash/latency diagnostics.

For more information on how Google uses data from partner apps, please review Google’s Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Non-Personalized Ads Fallback

If you decline personalized advertising consent or opt out where applicable, Starfleet Armada Pinball requests non-personalized ads (npa=1) from Google AdMob. Non-personalized ads do not use your Advertising ID for behavioral profiling or interest-based ad targeting, though contextual signals and coarse location/IP checks may still be used for frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Optional Rewarded Ads

Optional rewarded ads (such as continuing after Game Over, +1 Extra Ball, or Aegis Shield bonuses) are strictly user-initiated. Rewards are granted only upon verified completion of the rewarded ad (onUserEarnedReward).

4. Consent Management (Google UMP — GDPR & CCPA)

Starfleet Armada Pinball integrates Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to respect regional privacy regulations:

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR): On first launch in applicable regions, you will be presented with a consent dialog allowing you to accept, manage, or decline personalized advertising.
  • California & Applicable US States (CCPA / CPRA): Eligible users may opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising via the UMP privacy notice.
  • Manage Privacy Settings: You can review, modify, or withdraw your advertising consent at any time inside the Game’s Settings → Privacy Settings → Manage button, as well as through Android’s system Settings → Google → Ads menu (reset or delete Advertising ID).

5. In-App Purchases (“Remove Ads”)

Version 1.0 of Starfleet Armada Pinball launches free and ad-supported (no in-app purchases are active in v1.0). “Remove Ads” (remove_ads SKU) is an optional future/region-supported upgrade planned via Google Play Billing (v1.1+):

  • Where available and supported, payment methods, billing addresses, and receipts are handled directly by Google Play. We never receive or store your credit card or payment details.
  • When enabled and purchased, “Remove Ads” disables interstitial ads, lower-apron sponsor ads, and any banner placements. Optional player-initiated rewarded continues and bonuses remain available if you choose to watch them.

6. Children’s Privacy

Starfleet Armada Pinball is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party SDK, please contact us so we can address the issue promptly.
